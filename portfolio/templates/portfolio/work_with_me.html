Terms

Terms of Engagement

Payment

Fixed-price projects: 50% upfront, 50% on delivery. For larger projects, milestone-based payments.

Retainers: Invoiced monthly in advance. First month is pro-rated.

Accepted: M-Pesa, bank transfer (KES/USD), Payoneer, Wise.

Intellectual Property

All custom code I write for you becomes your property upon final payment.

I may use the project in my portfolio (screenshots, description) unless you request otherwise in writing.

I retain the right to reuse generic utilities and patterns I build as part of my standard toolkit.

Confidentiality

I treat all client information as confidential by default. I will sign an NDA if required.

I do not share client code, business logic, or proprietary data with third parties.

Scope Changes

For fixed-price projects, any change outside the agreed scope is documented in a Change Order with additional cost and timeline impact.

Small clarifications (under 2 hours of work) are absorbed without formal change orders.

Communication

I'm async-first. I respond to messages within 4 business hours (EAT timezone).

Weekly written status updates for all active engagements. Video calls by appointment.

Termination

Either party may terminate with 2 weeks' written notice.

Upon termination, I deliver all completed work. Work-in-progress is billed at the agreed rate for hours completed.
